Stage 7.6.2 scheme fix

Stage 7.6 failed before compiling the UI test target because xcodebuild could not find the PikminPilotRunnerUITests scheme.

After xcodegen generate, Stage 7.6.2 now:
1. parses project.pbxproj for the actual app and UI-test target UUIDs,
2. writes a deterministic shared PikminPilotRunnerUITests.xcscheme,
3. verifies both targets and the scheme with xcodebuild -list -json,
4. only then runs build-for-testing.

Phone-local RSD/DTX and runner-discovery logic are unchanged.
